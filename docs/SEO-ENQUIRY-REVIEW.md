# SEO and enquiry review — 23 October 2026

Pre-release review record. See `SEO-PUBLICATION.md` for the subsequent authorized release and remaining access requirements.

## Implemented

- Extended the existing Vite static rendering pipeline rather than replacing the framework. Published blog HTML/Markdown is sanitized and rendered in full, with shared title removal and Markdown conversion in the browser. Added profile guidance, study levels, sources, course links, course modules and official-source links. Scholarship pages include full descriptions, eligibility, conditions, steps, funding, dates and source links.
- Course, university and scholarship metadata/schema helpers are shared with React. Real course codes distinguish catalogue variants in both environments. Scholarship markup describes a grant rather than inventing a university partnership or treating the scholarship itself as a degree programme. Closed/archived scholarship pages retain their canonical, have noindex, and are excluded from the sitemap.
- Directories include introductions and crawlable links. The UK planning page links existing UK institutions to their courses and enquiry next steps. Articles link to relevant UK planning and visa guidance when their titles/tags support that context, plus courses, universities, scholarships and consultation. Scholarship cards now expose detail links alongside the existing interactions.
- `/free-consultation` now asks for a name, one selected contact method, destination and optional question, plus processing consent. It describes the existing lead-save and admissions follow-up workflow without a response-time promise. Receipt, failure, retained answers, retry and duplicate-submit protection are included. `/intake-form` retains the full academic/application workflow.
- The existing `submit-website-lead` function accepts the short enquiry and still enforces full-intake contact validation. Nullable contact columns already exist, so no database migration is required. Both enquiries and intakes continue through `website_leads` and follow-up `tasks`, with consent, rate limiting, honeypot and duplicate checks retained. Phone-only submissions do not fabricate email addresses.
- Full intake defaults to next January, filters past dated terms, resets the term when the intake year changes, and rejects stale selections during validation. Rolling intake remains available without asserting a fixed start date.
- Featured university cards link to internal profiles, including Chester, Wrexham and York St John. Unbacked fallback cards link to the directory rather than fabricated profiles. Profiles link to course details and consultation and retain the distinction between a directory listing and a formal partnership.
- Centralized registered-office information in `src/config/business.ts`. [Companies House company 16172129](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16172129) lists **54 Blakey Close, Redcar, England, TS10 4PB** (address change AD01 filed 19 October 2026), which supersedes the older Office 10 Seagreen Turner Street, TS10 1AZ address. This is explicitly a registered office, not a walk-in consultation promise.
- Removed public blog promises of weekly publication: the 11 published posts run from 13 July through 18 September 2026, with gaps. No article dates or stored content were changed.
- Removed global high-priority video preload and early buffering. Mobile, reduced-motion and data-saving visitors keep the poster. Eligible desktop video starts after a delay; the 12 MB video no longer competes with initial content on every route. Non-home prerenders do not preload the hero poster.
- Added `enquiry_start` and `enquiry_submit_success` hooks to the optional existing `gtag` interface. They contain only fixed event/form names, install no tracker and queue nothing when no tracker is present. They do not use the existing database analytics helper, which captures URL/user information. No form values, reference codes or personal data are passed to these events.
- Repaired the missing lockfile entries so `npm ci` succeeds, and excluded generated browser caches/reports from lint and version control.
